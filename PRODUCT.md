# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

NMSU students across disciplines, as well as sponsors, employers, and others interested in the club. The site helps visitors discover the club, learn about its activities, and find ways to connect.

## Product Purpose

The Cybersecurity Club at NMSU supports practical cybersecurity learning through hands-on activities, challenges, and community. Its website makes the club and its activities discoverable and helps visitors connect with it.

## Operating Context

Visitors can find information about meetings, events, workshops, and Capture the Flag challenges, and connect through the club's Crimson Connection profile, Discord, Instagram, or email. Meeting times and locations can change by semester.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the current club name and supplied brand assets.
- Verify meeting and event details before publishing them; direct visitors to current club channels when details are unconfirmed.
- The site is an existing Astro web project.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name Cybersecurity Club at NMSU and preserve the supplied club brand assets in `public/`.

## Product Principles

- Help visitors find practical ways to learn, participate, and connect with the club.
- Welcome students across disciplines and support the broader audiences interested in the club.
- Distinguish confirmed information from unverified or time-sensitive details.
- Preserve the club's existing identity and authentic source material.
